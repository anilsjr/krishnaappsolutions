2. Summary

  • The App is a hydration tracker and reminder tool.
  • Most of your data (water logs, goals, and preferences) is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

  • We do not sell your personal information.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide in the App (stored locally on your device)

When you use the App, the following information may be stored on your device to provide core functionality:

  • Hydration logs: drink volume, beverage type, and timestamp of each entry.
  • Health-related inputs (optional): sex, body weight, and activity level used to calculate a suggested daily water goal.
  • Goals and preferences: daily hydration target, volume unit (ml/oz), reminder interval, quiet hours, mute status, selected beverage, theme (light/dark), and language.
  • Onboarding status: whether you completed initial setup.

This information is considered sensitive when it relates to your health or fitness (for example, weight and hydration habits). It is used only to operate the App on your device.

3.2 Automatically collected information

  • App usage counters (local): launch counts and prompt timing for in-app review and share features, stored on your device.
  • Device time zone: used to schedule hydration reminders at the correct local time.
  • Notification permission status: whether you allowed the App to send reminders.

3.3 Information collected by third parties

When ads are displayed, Google AdMob may collect or receive:

  • Advertising ID (AAID on Android)
  • IP address (which may indicate approximate location)
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • App bundle ID and ad unit identifiers

Google may use this data to serve, measure, and personalize ads, and to prevent fraud. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

When you tap links inside the App (for example, Privacy Policy or Play Store), your browser or the Play Store app may collect data according to their own policies.

The App may download fonts from Google Fonts when connected to the internet. Google may receive standard technical data (such as IP address) as part of that request.

3.4 Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or postal address through normal App use.
  • We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • We do not operate user accounts or cloud backup of your hydration data in the current version of the App.
  • We do not currently use Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics in the App.

If you email us for support, we will receive the information you choose to include in your message (such as your email address and message content).

4. How we use your information

  • Track daily water intake and show progress, charts, and streaks.
  • Calculate and adjust your hydration goal based on your optional inputs.
  • Send local hydration reminder notifications at times you configure.
  • Remember your settings (theme, language, units, reminders).
  • Display advertisements through Google AdMob to support the free App.
  • Offer optional in-app review and share features.
  • Respond to support requests you send us by email.
  • Comply with applicable laws and protect against misuse or fraud.

We do not use your locally stored hydration or health-related data for advertising profiling.

  2. Tap Storage → Clear data (or Clear storage).
