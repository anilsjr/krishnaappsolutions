Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026Publisher: KRISHNAPPS

Privacy at a glance: Tic Tac Toe does not require an account, display ads, or offer subscriptions. Game data is stored locally on your device. The app may use local notifications to remind you about gameplay or app events when you grant notification permission.

1. Introduction

Tic Tac Toe is a mobile game published by KRISHNAPPS. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the game. The game is designed to operate primarily on your device and does not require registration or an account.

2. Information We Collect

KRISHNAPPS does not collect or transmit personal information, gameplay data, or device identifiers to developer-operated servers through Tic Tac Toe. The game does not use advertising services, analytics services, subscription services, or third-party tracking technologies.

3. Local Game Data

To provide gameplay, the app may store information in local application storage on your device, such as:

  • Game preferences and settings
  • Game results, scores, or win and loss records
  • Selected game mode or difficulty
  • Sound and notification preferences

This information remains on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to KRISHNAPPS. Android may back up or restore app data depending on your device and Google account settings. Those services are managed by Android and their respective providers.

4. Local Notifications

Tic Tac Toe may schedule local notifications on your device for gameplay reminders or other app-related events. These notifications are generated by the app or operating system on your device and are not sent from a KRISHNAPPS server.

Notification permission is controlled by your device's operating system. You can allow, disable, or manage notifications at any time through your device settings. Notification preferences may be stored locally with the app's settings.

5. Advertising and Subscriptions

Tic Tac Toe does not display advertisements and does not offer subscriptions or recurring payments. The app does not use advertising identifiers or ad networks.

6. Permissions

The app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or advertising identifier. If notification access is requested, it is used only to show local notifications as described in this policy. Any system capabilities included by the operating system are controlled by your device settings.

7. Sharing and External Services

Tic Tac Toe does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. If the app provides a link to another app or service, that service may handle information under its own privacy policy. KRISHNAPPS does not control the privacy practices of external services.

8. Children's Privacy

Tic Tac Toe does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. The game does not include chat, user uploads, targeted advertising, or account registration. Because the app does not collect personal information, no personal profile is created for players.

9. Retention and Deletion

Local game data and preferences generally remain on your device until you clear the app's storage, reset the data, or uninstall the app. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings. Android backups may retain copies of app data separately; those backups can be managed through your device or Google account settings.

10. Data Security

Tic Tac Toe is designed to operate locally without sending player progress or personal information to developer-operated servers. The security of locally stored data also depends on the security of your device, operating system, and account settings.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the game's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
