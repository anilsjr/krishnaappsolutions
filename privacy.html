Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 2026

(A) Who we are

We are KRISHH APPS SOLUTIONS, a mobile application publishing company based in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India. We are the controller of your personal information and responsible for how your data is collected and used. In this policy, "we", "our", or "us" refers to KRISHH APPS SOLUTIONS.

(B) Our values and purpose

We value your privacy and are committed to transparency. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user of our Android applications published on Google Play.

(C) Legal basis for processing

We process personal data based on your consent (where required), our legitimate interests in improving and operating our apps, and compliance with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us or adjusting in-app settings where available.

(D) Information we collect

  • Device & Usage Data: Device type, OS version, app usage data, IP address, and approximate location.
  • Advertising Data: If allowed, Advertising IDs (AAID/IDFA) and interaction with ads. We participate in Google AdMob; our app-ads.txt file is published at the root of our website for ad verification.

  • Support Data: Email address and any information you provide when contacting support.

(E) How we use your information

We use your data to improve app performance, show relevant advertisements, respond to support queries, fix bugs or crashes, and develop new features. We do not sell your personal information.

(F) Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Analytics and advertising data may be retained according to third-party provider policies.

(G) Third-party services

We may share limited data with third-party services for advertising (including Google AdMob), analytics, and crash reporting. These services may collect device identifiers and usage data independently under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of Google and other integrated services.

(H) International transfers

Your information may be processed and stored on servers located outside your country of residence, including in countries that may have different data protection laws. We take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place when data is transferred internationally.

(I) Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access your data, request correction or deletion, withdraw consent, or object to processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at satish.vishwakarma.it@gmail.com.

(J) Children's privacy

Our apps are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.
