Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026Publisher: KRISHNAPPS

Privacy at a glance: Ninja Hero works without an account. It stores game progress locally and does not send personal information, gameplay data, or device identifiers to servers operated by KRISHNAPPS. The game has no ads, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or real-money purchases.

1. Introduction

Ninja Hero is an Android game published by KRISHNAPPS. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the game. Ninja Hero operates on your device without requiring registration or login.

2. Information Collection

Ninja Hero does not collect or transmit personal information, gameplay data, or device identifiers to developer-operated servers. The game does not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs and does not offer real-money purchases.

3. Local Game Data

To support gameplay, Ninja Hero saves the following information in local application storage on your device:

  • Unlocked levels and game progress
  • Best scores and star ratings
  • Retry counts
  • Character unlocks and selected character
  • Virtual coins
  • Sound and music preferences

Older saved games may also contain legacy lives and cooldown values. These no longer restrict play. Locally stored game data is not transmitted to KRISHNAPPS.

Android may back up or restore app data depending on your device and Google account settings. These services are managed by Android and their respective providers.

4. Sharing and External Links

Share: The Share button opens your device's sharing interface with a public link to the game. KRISHNAPPS does not access your contacts or receive information about the recipients you choose.

More Apps: The More Apps button opens Google Play outside the game. Your chosen sharing application and Google Play may handle information under their own privacy policies. You can read Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions

Ninja Hero does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or advertising identifier. System-level permissions or capabilities included by audio and sharing components are used to support those features, not to collect personal information for KRISHNAPPS.

6. Children's Privacy

Ninja Hero does not request players' names, birth dates, or contact information. It does not include chat, user uploads, or targeted advertising. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Retention and Deletion

Local game progress and preferences generally remain until you clear Ninja Hero's app storage or uninstall the game. Android backups may retain copies separately; manage these through your device or account settings. KRISHNAPPS does not maintain game accounts or server-side player profiles to delete.

8. Data Security

Ninja Hero is designed to operate locally without sending player progress or personal information to developer-operated servers. Security of local data also depends on your device and Android security settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the game's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
